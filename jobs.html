Meet Tommy.

"Hey I am Thomas! I graduated from the University of Georgia in 2018, I began working in the finance world. Then, quickly I realized I was meant for something more. In 2020, I visited Refuge to see my best friend, Chase Adams, and this sparked a new direction. I joined the team as a guide and quickly found purpose in building meaningful relationships and serving others. Now as Staff Recruiter, I am passionate about connecting with men and women who feel called to invest in people and live with purpose while serving incredible men and women who are doing important work in their communities."
