Women's Trips

Refuge Women's Trips create space for women serving in ministry, nonprofits, and leadership to step away from everyday demands, reconnect with God, build meaningful relationships, and return home renewed.

If you have been invited on a Refuge Trip:

Refuge operates through a host model. If you've been invited by a host, register using your invitation or the registration link below.

What To Expect

There is no packed itinerary. Days are intentionally flexible so guests can enjoy outdoor activities, meaningful conversations, or quiet rest. The only consistent gathering each evening is dinner followed by Table Talk. Fly-fishing on the Bighorn River, fireside conversations, shared meals, riverside reflection, and epic landscapes form the backdrop. But what actually happens is deeper: women finally exhale, lower their guard, reconnect with God, and discover where they need to do some honest work in their lives. Every night, guests gather around the table and find clarity, connection, and the courage to act.

Women's Trips take place January through Easter at our Montana location:

Montana — The Bluffs Lodge at Fort Smith sits on a bluff overlooking the winding Bighorn River, with rooms for 17 guests. The lodge offers world-class fly-fishing, a wood-burning fireplace, sauna, large outdoor fire pit, and indoor and outdoor spaces designed for rest, reflection, and meaningful conversation.
