Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Trip registration: your name, email address, mobile phone number, street address, emergency contact name and phone number, trip dates, field of leadership, travel method, allergy and special-needs information you choose to share, and your acceptance of our waiver.

  • Newsletter: your email address and name.
  • Post-trip survey: your survey responses and, if you ask for one, your request for a giving reminder and your separate consent to receive text messages (see “Text messages” below).
  • Donations: when you give through our online giving page, your payment is handled by our donation platform, Givebutter. We receive your donor information (such as name, email, and gift amount). [CONFIRM we never receive full card numbers]

Text messages (SMS)

We only send donation reminder texts to guests who request a giving reminder in our post-trip survey and separately agree to receive text messages. We do not send general marketing updates or trip promotions by text. Your consent is not a condition of attending a trip or making a donation.

We do not share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Your mobile number and consent record are shared only with the service providers that deliver our texts, as described below. You can stop texts at any time by replying STOP. See our SMS Terms of Service for full details.
