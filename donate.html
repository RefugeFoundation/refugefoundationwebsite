Ways to Donate

Help Keep World Changers In The Game.

Every donation helps create spaces where leaders can rest, reconnect, and return renewed through intentional hospitality and community. Refuge is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit — your gift is tax-deductible to the fullest extent permitted by law.

Give Online

The fastest way to give — one-time or monthly, any amount. You'll be taken to our secure giving page hosted by Givebutter to complete your gift.

Donate

Your Gift Changes the Trajectory of a Leader's Life.

4 out of 5 leaders are healthier now because of Refuge. 1 in 5 pastors who visited Refuge say they would have quit ministry if not for their time here. Since 2007, Refuge has served over 5,000 leaders whose ministries and organizations collectively influence 16.4 million people.

Your generosity helps us cover the cost of lodging, meals, fly-fishing, and staff who lavishly serve every guest — so leaders can focus on rest, connection, and clarity instead of an invoice.
