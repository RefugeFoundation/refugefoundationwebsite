Refuge Foundation

Keeping World Changers in the Game.

Refuge creates space for pastors, nonprofit leaders, and world changers to step away from the noise, experience meaningful connection, and return home renewed through immersive outdoor hospitality in Montana and Wyoming.

Donate

Who takes care of the people who spend their lives taking care of everyone else?

For years, Refuge Founder Brian Carpenter spent time with pastors, parents, and marketplace leaders across the country. Behind their passion and commitment, he noticed a common reality: many were exhausted. They carried the weight of their churches, organizations, families, and communities, yet few had a place where they could simply be cared for.

That realization became the foundation of Refuge.

Not another conference.
Not another leadership event.
A place where leaders could rest, build genuine relationships, spend time outdoors, and leave encouraged to continue the work they had been called to do.
