SMS Terms of Service

Last updated: October 9, 2026

These terms apply to the text-message program run by Refuge Foundation (“Refuge,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). Please also read our Privacy Policy.

What you will receive

Refuge sends donation reminder text messages only to guests who request a giving reminder in our post-trip survey and separately consent to receive texts. We do not send general marketing updates or trip promotions by text.

How you opt in

In our post-trip survey you can ask for a giving reminder and, separately, check a box agreeing to receive those reminders by text at the mobile number you provide. The box is not pre-checked. Consent is not a condition of attending a trip or making a donation.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply HELP for help, or STOP to opt out. After you reply STOP you will get one confirmation message and no further texts unless you opt in again. You can also contact us at info@refugefoundation.org or (406) 501-5495.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your information.

We do not share SMS opt-in data or text-messaging consent with third parties for marketing purposes. Your mobile number is shared only with the providers that deliver our texts, including HubSpot and its messaging partners.
